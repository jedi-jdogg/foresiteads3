# Foresite BD Pipeline

A living business-development pipeline for Foresite Ads: every prospect, customer, partner and referral source from the last 12 months of Gmail, Calendly, the Santiago/Jonathan pipeline sheet and the Foresite platform, scored into high / medium / low priority with a recommended next action, and a hosted tool where the team edits it and emails from it.

## What is here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pipeline.html` | The tool. Published as a claude.ai Artifact with the `db` capability (edits are saved for everyone), the Gmail connector (send emails and team updates from the page, check a contact's inbox history) and CSV export. |
| `data/seed.json` | The merged, scored pipeline that seeds the tool's database. Regenerated by the script below. |
| `data/sheet_pipeline.json` | Normalized copy of the "Foresite AI Pipeline - Jonathan / Santiago" Google Sheet. |
| `data/foresite_platform.json` | Foresite platform tenants and subscription states (customers, churn, signups that never subscribed). |
| `data/sources/*.json` | Raw sweeps of Gmail labels, sent mail and Calendly produced by Claude (see the skill). |
| `data/overrides.json` | Optional hand edits keyed by company that win over every source. |
| `scripts/build_seed.py` | Merges and dedupes every source, maps stages, scores priority, writes `data/seed.json` and `reports/<date>-bd-pipeline.md`. |
| `reports/` | Dated pipeline analysis reports. |

## How priority is scored

Only open stages score; closed-won, active customers and lost deals score 0.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Stage: new 5 · intro 10 · call scheduled 25 · call done 30 · proposal/contract 40 · negotiating 42 · stalled 8 | stage weight |
| Size: revenue > $20M or spend > $100K/mo +22 · > $5M or > $25K/mo +16 · > $1M +9 · unknown +6 | size |
| Recency of last contact: ≤7d +15 · ≤30d +10 · ≤90d +4 · >120d −5 | recency |
| They wrote last | +10 |
| Meeting booked in the future | +18 |
| Next action overdue | +4 |
| Partner / channel deal | +6 |

High ≥ 60, medium ≥ 38, low otherwise. The tool shows the suggestion on every deal and lets you override it.

## Refreshing

1. Ask Claude to re-run the BD pipeline sweep (skill `.claude/skills/bd-pipeline`). It rewrites `data/sources/*.json`.
2. `python3 scripts/build_seed.py` regenerates `data/seed.json` and the dated report.
3. Claude republishes `pipeline.html` with `seed.json` alongside it and seeds any new deals into the artifact database without overwriting edits made in the tool.

Stages used everywhere: `new`, `intro`, `call_scheduled`, `discovery`, `proposal`, `negotiating`, `won`, `customer`, `stalled`, `lost`.
