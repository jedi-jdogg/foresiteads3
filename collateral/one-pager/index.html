How it works · one operator, six systems, one number

Buyers don't decide in one click. Foresite surrounds the decision.

They see you on mobile, compare on a laptop, and buy a week later. Foresite sits above your existing stack, identifies who arrived, and coordinates channel, message, audience and timing for each of them, all scored on one blended number: total revenue over total spend, nothing double-counted.

The revenue loop

Five stages, run continuously. Olivia sits in the middle, and stage 5 feeds stage 2.

1 · Connect

Plug in, no engineering

Native Shopify app plus your Google, Meta, TikTok and Bing accounts, Klaviyo, GA4 and CTV. Up and running in hours.

2 · Identify

Turn visitors into buyers

A first-party pixel and identity-resolution partners recognize 18–57% of visitors. Each profile carries intent, stage and history.

3 · Decide

Olivia picks the next move

Daily per-client metrics for ads, flows and CTV roll into one blended ROAS scorecard, with first-order profit modeled for your brand.

4 · Act

Every channel at once

Budget moves, new creative, a flow, a push, a CTV audience or an AEO article: written from your brand profile and executed together.

5 · Measure

Learn and repeat

Nothing double-counted. Outcomes and full execution logs feed back into the profile, so results compound with tenure.

What a channel tool cannot bolt on

  • Identity graph. Pixel plus resolution partners recognize 18–57% of visitors on live sites, versus 0–21% without. Profiles feed every system.
  • Blended attribution layer. One scorecard across ads, flows and CTV, with first-order profitability modeled per brand.
  • Olivia's agent platform. Production agents with scoped permissions and full execution and audit logs. They manage budgets, generate creative and run 600+ live flows.
  • Brand marketing profile. A versioned profile of your voice, offers and audiences drives creative, copy and flow generation, so quality improves with tenure.
  • Cross-channel outcome data. 12+ months of ads, flows and CTV results across 50+ brands: the training set a single-channel tool cannot assemble.
