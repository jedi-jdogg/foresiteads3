Who takes what

Each play is a kind of work. The words under it are what the hub listens for. The play with the most matching words wins; its first choice takes the request, or the first backup that is set up. Plays with a council offer one when they match.

Type to see which play it hits and who would take it.

Edit agent-hub/playbook.json to change who takes a play or to add words. The hub reloads it on restart.

What actually worked

Every 👍 and 👎 on an answer lands here under the play that routed it. When an agent keeps losing on a play, move a backup up in the playbook.
