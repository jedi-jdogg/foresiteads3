Connected apps (via Claude)

Type what you need. The playbook decides which agent fits, and the line under the box shows its pick before you send.

  • Auto: follows the playbook. A clear fit goes straight through; a close call shows you the options.
  • Smart: Claude reads the playbook and your request and decides.
  • Council: up to three agents answer, then Claude merges them and flags disagreements.
  • Direct: click an agent on the left to talk to it alone, with follow-ups.

Rate answers with 👍 / 👎. The ratings show which agent really does best at each kind of work.
