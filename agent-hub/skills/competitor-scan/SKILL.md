# Competitor scan

Use this for "what is <brand> doing", "competitor check", "scan the competition".

1. Live research first: current promotions, new products, pricing changes, and press from the last 30 days. Cite sources.
2. Social pulse: what people are saying about them on X and Reddit this month, and the recurring complaint.
3. Ads: the offers and hooks in their current Meta ads (Meta Ad Library) and the keywords they appear on in search.
4. End with "Copy", "Counter" and "Ignore": one or two items each, with the reason.
5. Under 300 words. Dates on every claim.
